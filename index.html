🇮🇳 How to Play on a Laptop (Trackpad & Keys) + Pickleball Rules

🖱️ 1. Easy Laptop Trackpad Controls

• Move Finger on Trackpad: Aims your green target ring on court AND automatically glides your player to the ball!
• 1-Finger Tap / Click (or SPACE): Plays a Normal Shot / Serve (with Smart Auto-Shot!).
• 2-Finger Tap / Right-Click (or Key L): Hits a High Lob / Drop Shot over the net.
• 2-Finger Scroll Up / Down: Adds Topspin (↑) or Backspin Slice (↓)!

⌨️ 2. Simple Keyboard Shortcuts

• SPACE: Serve or Hit the ball.
• 1 (or D): Normal Fast Drive • 2 (or K): Soft Net Dink
• 3 (or L): High Lob / Drop Arc • 4 (or V): Quick Net Volley
• 5 (or M): Overhead Smash • 6 (or X): Backspin Slice
• WASD / Arrow Keys: Manual court footwork.

⏳ 3. The Two-Bounce Rule & The Kitchen

• Two-Bounce Rule: The serve AND the return of serve must each bounce once before anyone can hit the ball out of the air. Watch the floating prompt above your player—it tells you when to wait for the bounce!
• The Kitchen (7ft Light-Blue Zone): Never hit a ball in the air while standing inside the light-blue Kitchen zone. Let it bounce first!

🏆 4. How Pickleball Scoring Works (0 - 0 - 2)

• Only the Serving Team can score points (games to 11, win by 2).
• In Doubles, the score has 3 numbers: Your Score – AI Score – Server # (1 or 2).
• Matches start at 0 - 0 - 2. Check the top-left & top-center Live Scoreboard at any time!

💡 Beginner Tip: Keep 🐢 Pace: Easy (75%), 🤖 Smart Shot: AUTO, and 🖱️ Trackpad Move: ON enabled in the top bar—then all you have to do is move your finger on the trackpad and Tap / Click when the ball comes to your side!
