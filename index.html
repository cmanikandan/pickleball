📖 Official USAPA Pickleball Rules & On-Screen Controls

1. The Non-Volley Zone ("The Kitchen")

The 7-foot zone on both sides of the net is the Non-Volley Zone. Players may never hit a volley (a ball out of the air before it bounces) while touching the Kitchen or its 2-inch boundary lines—including via momentum after contact. You may step into the Kitchen at any time to hit a ball that has bounced.

2. The Double-Bounce (Two-Bounce) Rule

Every rally requires two mandatory bounces before volleying begins: (1) The receiver must let the serve bounce once in their service box, and (2) the serving team must let the return of serve bounce once on their side. Use key [3] 3rd-Shot Drop after Bounce #2 to safely advance to the Kitchen line!

3. Legal Underhand Serve

Serves must be struck underhand with an upward pendulum arc, paddle contact below the waist (navel), and the paddle head below the wrist. The serve must clear the Kitchen (including the NVZ line) and land in the diagonal crosscourt service box.

4. Side-Out Scoring & Doubles (0-0-2)

Only the serving team can score points (games to 11, win by 2). In Doubles, the score has 3 numbers: Serving Team – Receiving Team – Server # (1 or 2). Matches start at 0-0-2 so the first lost rally triggers an immediate Side-Out. On points won, serving partners switch Left/Right sides.
